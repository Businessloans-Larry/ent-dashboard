Definitions & data notes
Workable Leads
Excludes these unqualified reasons: Fake App, Fraud, Data Merch, Previous Default, Bad Contact Info.
Inbound Completed Leads
Came in with signature — completed_application = true, not attributed to the SDR campaign.
Incomplete SDR Leads
Leads matched to the SDR / incomplete campaign 878f46ba-a635-4536-afdc-d2adc82569aa ("Direct – Enterprise Retransmit").
SDR Completed App %
SDR completed apps as a percentage of that campaign's total, excluding leads dispositioned Fake App or Bad Contact Info.
Win Rate / In Month Win Rate
Units ÷ Approvals. "In month" counts only deals funded in the same calendar month the lead was created.
Avg. Maturity
Average days from signed to funded. Cells with a dotted gold underline cover only part of that month's funded deals — hover for the count.
RPL
Total Revenue ÷ Direct Leads — the same formula the Tableau dashboard used, reproduced to the cent against its 9/1/26 snapshot. Both sides are the created-month cohort: the revenue is what leads created that month went on to earn, whenever they funded, so it is a true return per lead. It matures late. A cohort has booked ~63% of its eventual revenue at the end of its own month, ~95% thirty days later and ~99% after sixty. So the newest month reads low, does most of its catching up within thirty days, and is not finished until about sixty. The gate lets a month in at the thirty-day mark, still roughly 5% light, rather than holding it two months for the rest. Above each quarter it pools revenue over leads across that quarter’s completed months rather than averaging the monthly rates.
Channel Breakdown
Derived from utm_source / utm_medium on the deal. Newsletter is utm_source=newsletter or utm_medium=crm.
Unattributed
Both utm_source and utm_medium are empty, so the channel is genuinely unknown — not the same as "direct". Per Shane Waters: typically an ad blocker stripping the tags, or someone who saw a social ad and then came to BusinessLoans.com organically. Roughly 8% of leads (231 of 2,809 in 2026). Treat it as missing data, not as its own channel — some share of it belongs to paid social and paid search.
Pacing
Current month's run rate: month-to-date ÷ days elapsed × days in month. Tiles turn red when that pace is below the year-to-date monthly average.
% Called and % Contacted are not shown. Those come from call-activity data (JustCall / Zoho Calls) that the Zoho data connector behind this page does not expose, so they are left out rather than estimated.
